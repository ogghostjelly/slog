Code Soup Philosophy

How to grow a website from hydrothermal soup vents.

No DRY. get WET. Don't don't repeat yourself.
No abstractions. Copy and paste.
No generalisations. Localised changes only. Nothing affects everything.
No static. Everything changes all the time.
No good websites. Living is ugly and broken. Let it live.
No unknown. It's about control, understand it, change it.

This too will change.
